# SMFA-3D
SMFA 3D: lead The Smiley Face Army as President in a blocky voxel war game. Command squads from your office or fight on the front lines. Conquer the world in a story campaign with cutscenes, diplomacy, navy battles and stealth missions. Play skirmish, arcade or God Mode sandbox, unlock skins, earn medals and rewatch your best kills and crashes.
